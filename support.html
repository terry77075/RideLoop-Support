outabit Support

Help for the outabit beta on iPhone and Android. Beta access for both platforms is available by request.

iPhone beta with TestFlight

Install Apple’s TestFlight app first. Then reopen your outabit invitation email or invitation link, tap Accept, and tap Install. You do not need a redeem code. Beta access is handled by request; this page does not contain a public invitation link.

Android beta installation and updates

Use the Android installation guide for the approved download link, source-specific install permission, first open, and safe in-place updates. Beta access is handled by request.

Location permission and background tracking

outabit needs device location permission to record routes and calculate GPS-based activity information. Location and background settings on your phone can affect recording, especially while the screen is locked.

On Android, battery optimization can also interrupt background GPS recording. If a recording stops unexpectedly, check the location, background, and battery permissions available for outabit before the next activity.

Activity history and saved routes

Activity history and saved routes are separate. Removing activity history should not remove routes that you deliberately saved for future use. Before an update, keep copies of any exported activity or route information you cannot replace.

Need help?

Email support@outabit.com. Include your platform (iPhone or Android), device model, OS version, outabit version or build, what happened, and the steps that led to the issue. If a screenshot would help, remove private information before attaching it.
