RideLoop Support

Support information for RideLoop.

About RideLoop

RideLoop is a GPS activity tracker for cycling, walking, hiking, kayaking, and everyday exploring.

Location Permission

RideLoop needs location permission to record routes and calculate GPS-based activity information. For the best route recording, allow the level of location access requested by the app and make sure Location Services are enabled on your iPhone.

Background Tracking

GPS recording can be affected by iPhone permissions, battery settings, and whether the operating system allows RideLoop to continue receiving location updates while the screen is locked. RideLoop will continue to be improved as background-tracking support is refined.

Activity History and Saved Routes

Activity history and saved routes are separate. Removing activity history should not remove routes that you deliberately saved for future use.

Need Help?

A dedicated public RideLoop support contact will be added here before the public App Store release. Until then, this page serves as the support-information location for TestFlight and App Store preparation.
