RideLoop Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026

RideLoop is designed to track outdoor activities while minimizing collection of personal information.

Information RideLoop Uses

RideLoop may use your device location, motion or sensor information, and activity details when needed to record an activity and calculate information such as route, distance, speed, time, and elevation.

Location Data

Location access is used to provide GPS tracking and mapping features. RideLoop is designed to store recorded activity information on your device. Saved activity records may include route coordinates and location-based notes when you choose to save them.

Local Storage

RideLoop does not require a user account. Activity history, saved routes, preferences, notes, and related statistics are intended to be stored locally on the device unless a future feature clearly tells you otherwise.

Sharing and Selling of Data

RideLoop does not sell your personal information. RideLoop does not include advertising and is not designed to provide your saved activity history to advertisers or social networks.

Maps and Device Services

Some RideLoop features rely on services provided by your device or operating system, such as location services and map display. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies and settings.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete individual activity records within the app. RideLoop is also being designed to allow users to remove detailed location history while retaining aggregate statistics, or to reset activity history and statistics completely. Saved routes are managed separately from activity-history cleanup.

Children

RideLoop is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through an account or advertising system. The app does not require an account.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as RideLoop changes. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

A public RideLoop support contact will be listed on the RideLoop Support page before public App Store release.
