Install step by step

  1. Download. Tap the blue button above in your full browser. Continue only when the address is on github.com/terry77075/MyHealthData-Android-Releases.
  2. Find the APK. Open your browser's Downloads list, or open the Android Files app and choose Downloads. Look for MyHealthData.apk.
  3. Allow this source only if Android asks. Tap the APK. If Android says this source is not allowed, open the setting offered by Android and allow installs for the one browser or Files app you are using. Return to the APK and try again. You can turn that source permission off after installation.
  4. Read the warning. Android may warn that APK files can be harmful. Continue only if the GitHub source and filename above are correct. Otherwise, cancel.
  5. Install and open. Tap Install, then Open. Follow the app's first-open prompts and grant only permissions needed for features you choose.
Keep Android security protections on. Do not disable Play Protect or broadly allow unknown apps. If Android reports a signature, package, or other unexpected security problem, cancel and contact support.

Updating without losing local data

Use the same blue download button for future updates. Open the new APK and install it over the existing app. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling can remove the health records stored locally on your device.

Back up first. Use MyHealthData's Backup & Transfer feature before updating or moving to another phone. Store the backup and its recovery information safely and separately. Do not send a backup, recovery information, or health records to support unless you deliberately choose to share specific information.

First open and help

MyHealthData is a local recordkeeping app. On first open, follow the profile and privacy prompts shown by your installed version. You can enable the app's biometric lock after your device biometrics are set up.
