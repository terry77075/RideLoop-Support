MyHealthData - Local Only

Support

For help with MyHealthData - Local Only, email rideloopsupport@gmail.com. Please include “MyHealthData” in the subject so we can distinguish your request from RideLoop support.

Install or update

Use the Android installation guide for the fixed latest download link, source-specific install permission, backup caution, and safe in-place updates.

What to include

  • Your phone or tablet model and Android version.
  • The app version shown on the About screen.
  • What you were doing, what you expected, and the exact error message.
  • For Bluetooth problems, the device make and model.

Use a screenshot with personal information hidden if helpful. Do not post or email passwords, backups, recovery information, or unnecessary health information.

Unlocking the app

Set up device biometrics and a screen lock in Android Settings. If you enable Biometric lock in MyHealthData, the app uses Android's system authentication prompt; available fallback methods depend on your device.

Bluetooth readings

Enable Nearby devices or Bluetooth permission for MyHealthData, wake the health device, and keep it nearby. Bluetooth compatibility depends on the device model and measurement protocol. Discovery alone does not guarantee that readings can be imported. Manual entry remains available.

Medication doses and inventory

One medication can have several schedules with different quantities, all using its shared inventory. Record the amount actually taken or choose Skip. In versions with Taken at, adjust the date and time when recording a dose. Removing a medication from the lists preserves historical dose and inventory records.

Vaccination records and reminders

In versions that include Vaccinations, enter the date received and any next-dose date advised by your provider. Enable Remind me and choose the reminder date and time. Notifications must be allowed in Android Settings. The app does not calculate a vaccination schedule or assess immunity.

Importing a Walgreens vaccination PDF

Save the vaccination-history PDF to your device. In supported versions of MyHealthData, open Vaccinations, choose Import vaccination PDF, select the file, and review the vaccine names, dates, and provider details before adding them.

The first importer supports text-based Walgreens history PDFs. Scans and other provider layouts may require manual entry. The app skips same-vaccine/same-date duplicates.

Reports

Choose a measurement date range and use the report share button to print or share a PDF. The report summarizes your saved readings; it does not give a diagnosis or treatment recommendation.

Backup, data, and medical questions

Use Backup & Transfer before updating or moving to another phone. Keep the backup and recovery information safe and separate. The developer cannot recover health records stored only on your device. Read the privacy policy before deleting the app or sharing records. Contact your clinician or pharmacist about treatment, dose changes, and vaccination timing.
