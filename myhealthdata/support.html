MyHealthData - Local Only

Support

For help with MyHealthData - Local Only, email rideloopsupport@gmail.com. Please include “MyHealthData” in the subject so we can distinguish your request from RideLoop support.

What to include

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
  • The app version and build number shown in TestFlight, if applicable.
  • What you were doing, what you expected, and the exact error message.
  • For Bluetooth problems, the device make and model.

Use a screenshot with personal information hidden if helpful. Do not post or email passwords or unnecessary health information.

Unlocking the app

Set up Face ID or Touch ID on your device. The app requires enrolled device biometrics and uses the system authentication prompt; a system passcode fallback may be offered. It locks again when backgrounded.

Bluetooth readings

Enable Bluetooth permission for MyHealthData, wake the health device, and keep it nearby. Bluetooth compatibility depends on the device model and measurement protocol. Discovery alone does not guarantee that readings can be imported. Manual entry remains available.

Medication doses and inventory

One medication can have several schedules with different quantities, all using its shared inventory. Record the amount actually taken or choose Skip. In versions with Taken at, adjust the date and time when recording a dose. Removing a medication from the lists preserves historical dose and inventory records.

Vaccination records and reminders

In versions that include Vaccinations, enter the date received and any next-dose date advised by your provider. Enable Remind me and choose the reminder date and time. Notifications must be allowed in iOS Settings. Check the record's reminder status; later reminders may wait for scheduling capacity and are reconsidered when the app opens. The app does not calculate a vaccination schedule or assess immunity.

Importing a Walgreens vaccination PDF

Select your own profile in Walgreens, open vaccination records, then choose Share and Save to Files. In supported versions of MyHealthData, open Vaccinations, choose Import vaccination PDF, and select the file. Review the selected vaccine names, dates, and provider details before tapping Add.

The first importer supports text-based Walgreens history PDFs. It excludes the personalized-vaccine recommendations section and skips same-vaccine/same-date duplicates. Scans and other provider layouts may require manual entry. Direct import from the iOS share menu is not currently supported.

Reports

Choose a measurement date range and use the report share button to print or share a PDF. The optional Include vaccination record switch adds vaccination records from all dates. The report summarizes your saved readings; it does not give a diagnosis or treatment recommendation.

Data and medical questions

The developer cannot recover health records stored only on your device. Read the privacy policy before deleting the app or sharing records. Contact your clinician or pharmacist about treatment, dose changes, and vaccination timing. This support address is for app assistance and is not an emergency or clinical service.
