MyHealthData - Local Only

Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026

This policy describes MyHealthData - Local Only, developed by Terry Baldwin, including vaccination and PDF-import features as they become available. Features can vary by installed version.

Health information stays in the app's local database

MyHealthData does not require an account and does not operate a developer-hosted health-data service. The app stores the information you enter or import on your device. This may include measurements and dates, medication details, dose and inventory history, allergies, vaccination records, reminder dates, notes, an optional name for reports, and identifiers or technical details from Bluetooth devices you connect.

The local database is configured for SQLCipher encryption, with its key held in iOS secure storage. The app uses device authentication to protect access. MyHealthData does not receive your Face ID or Touch ID biometric templates.

What the developer collects

The app does not automatically send your health records to the developer. It does not include advertising, advertising identifiers, third-party behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell your personal information or use your health records for advertising.

Bluetooth, reminders, and selected files

Bluetooth permission is used to communicate with health devices you choose. Notification permission is used for local medication, inventory, and vaccination reminders. Reminder messages avoid displaying medication and vaccine names on the lock screen.

When you choose a supported vaccination PDF, the app extracts its text locally on the iPhone. You review and confirm the entries before they are saved. The importer attempts to delete its temporary cache copy after extraction and does not delete your original PDF. It does not upload the PDF to an AI service or developer server.

Sharing is your choice

Reports, PDFs, and diagnostic exports are created or shared when you choose those actions. Once you send a file through Mail, Messages, Files, a printer, or another service, the destination's privacy practices apply. Recipients can retain their own copies. Consider what a report contains before sharing it.

Apple and other services

Device backup, iCloud Drive, keyboard dictation, the App Store, TestFlight, and operating-system diagnostics are controlled by Apple and your settings. They may process or retain information independently of the app's local storage. In particular, TestFlight can provide Apple and the developer with testing feedback and crash or usage information. This policy does not override Apple's practices. The app does not provide its own cloud backup or synchronization service.

Keeping and removing records

Records remain locally until removed through an available app control or until the app's data is deleted. Removing a medication from the medication lists preserves its dose and inventory history. The seven-day dose-history view limits what is displayed; it does not erase older records. Removing a vaccination record also removes its configured reminder when notification reconciliation succeeds.

Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app may preserve data. Previously exported files, recipient copies, device backups, and secure-storage items can have separate retention rules. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase records stored only on your device and does not hold a recovery copy.

Support requests and this website

If you email rideloopsupport@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the content and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot the app. Email is handled through Google's email service. Support correspondence may be retained as needed to resolve requests and maintain support records; contact us to request deletion of correspondence we control.

Please avoid sending health records, passwords, full device serial numbers, or other sensitive information unless it is necessary for a support request. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information under GitHub's privacy practices. These pages do not add analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when these practices change. For privacy questions or requests, contact Terry Baldwin at rideloopsupport@gmail.com with the subject “MyHealthData privacy.”
