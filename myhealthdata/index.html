MyHealthData - Local Only

Your health records, organized on your device.

MyHealthData helps you keep home measurements, medication schedules, inventory, allergies, and vaccination records together without creating an app account.

Record readings manually or import them from supported Bluetooth devices. Review your history and choose when to print or share a report. Features can vary by installed version.

Download and install the latest public Android release

MyHealthData is a personal recordkeeping tool. It does not diagnose conditions, recommend treatment, or determine which vaccines you need.
